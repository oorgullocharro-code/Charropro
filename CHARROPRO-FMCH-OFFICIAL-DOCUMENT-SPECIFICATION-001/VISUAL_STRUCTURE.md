# Visual Structure

## Page characteristics

- Portrait single page with narrow outer margins.
- FMCH emblem at upper left; green federation heading across the top.
- Six header entry lines arranged in three horizontal rows.
- Nine score blocks stacked vertically, each with a gray title panel and a body table.
- Dotted boxes, thick outlined boxes, and three-cell control strips recur outside the primary data grids.
- Footer contains three judge signature areas, one captain signature area, CONADE mark, Secretariat period, and an institutional legend.

## Visual conventions recorded, not interpreted

- Red text is used in headers such as `MALOS` and in visible dash defaults.
- Gray fill appears on titles, total cells, and final score boxes.
- Dotted boxes are visually distinct from thick outlined boxes but their sports/control purpose is not printed in every instance.
- Blank horizontal cells under section headings are treated as data-entry positions only when their alignment makes that inference reasonable.
