# Section Inventory

## Field notation

The dictionary expands every repeated visual cell into a separate field record. No row, attempt, pass, score column, control cell, or signature box is fused into a shared definition. For example, Coleadero's first-pass cells are separately represented from `ROW_01.GOOD` through `ROW_04.TOTAL`.

| Section | Visible entry structure | Repetition and controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header | Event, time, team, date, captain, venue | One each |
| Cala | Participant, substitute, meters/times, Base, P, T, LD, LI, MD, MI, PC, Total, Malos grid, Total Malos, Puntos Parciales | Side dotted `Suma Puntos Malos`; one team infraction |
| Piales | Participant, substitute, 1st/2nd/3rd Good-Malos-Total, Total | Side dotted control; three lower unlabeled outlined cells; one infraction |
| Coleadero | Four participant rows, substitute, three pass groups Good-Malos-Total, Total | Side `Suma Control`, four lower dotted controls, three outlined cells, one infraction |
| Toro | Participant, substitute, thirteen labelled score columns | Side dotted control, time, three outlined controls, one infraction |
| Terna | Three participant rows, substitute, two Base/Adicionales and two Remate columns, Malos, T, Total | Side dotted control, time, right-side blank control, three outlined controls, one infraction |
| Yegua | Participant, substitute, thirteen labelled score columns | Side dotted control, time, three outlined controls, one infraction |
| Manganas Pie | Participant, substitute, three Good-Malos-Total groups, T, Total | Side dotted control, time, three outlined controls, one infraction |
| Manganas Caballo | Participant, substitute, three Good-Malos-Total groups, T, Total | Side dotted control, time, three outlined controls, one infraction |
| Paso | Participant, substitute, two Base values, Distance, Cuarta, Reparos, Oreja C/P, Malos, Total | Side dotted control, time out, three outlined controls, one infraction |
| Closing | `TOTAL, PUNTOS MALOS`; `PUNTUACIÓN FINAL` | One emphasized cell each |
| Signatures | Three `JUEZ`, one `CAPITÁN` | One blank signature line per label |
| Footer | CONADE mark/text, `SECRETARÍA DEL DEPORTE 2024 - 2028`, quote | Display-only |

No participant-role name is printed above several blank entry rows. Those fields are deliberately tagged `STRUCTURAL_INFERENCE` rather than attributed to a named role.
